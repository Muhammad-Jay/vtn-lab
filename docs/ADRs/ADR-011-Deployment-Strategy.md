# ADR-011: Deployment Strategy

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

The platform requires inexpensive and simple infrastructure for V1.

---

## Decision

Frontend:

- Vercel.

Backend:

- Render.

Database:

- Neon.

---

## Consequences

Benefits:

- Fast deployment.
- Low operational overhead.

Tradeoffs:

- Vendor dependencies.

Future considerations:

- Migration to self-hosted infrastructure remains possible.
